# Do Goldfish Really Forget? | Science | art:fish | Check if a claim is true
about: Ella's cousin says goldfish forget everything in three seconds, so Ella checks good sources to find out.
words: goldfish, memory, check, source, fact
cover: A girl kneels beside a fish tank, holding a library book about fish.
read: Find the claim at the start and the two sources Ella used. What did she find out?
Ella’s cousin Jake stared at her new goldfish, Sunny. “Goldfish have a three-second memory,” he said. “Sunny forgets everything right away.” | Jake taps the glass of a fish tank.
Ella felt sad. Did Sunny forget her every three seconds? Did he forget his home? | Ella looks worried at the tank.
“How do you know?” she asked. Jake shrugged. “Everyone says it. I saw it in a video.” | Jake holds up a tablet.
Ella frowned. Many people saying something did not make it a fact. She decided to check. | Ella crosses her arms, thinking.
Mom said, “A good source is a place you can trust to tell the truth. Let’s find two.” | Mom points to a bookshelf.
First, they went to the library. The librarian helped Ella find a book about fish, written by a scientist. | A librarian hands Ella a book.
The book said that scientists have trained goldfish. The fish learned to swim to one spot when they heard a sound, and got food there. | A drawing of a fish swimming to a bell sound.
Months later, the fish still swam to the right place when they heard the sound. Their memory lasted a long time! | A calendar and a fish swimming to the same spot.
For the second source, they visited an aquarium. Ella asked a fish keeper about goldfish memory. | Ella talks with a fish keeper beside a big tank.
“Goldfish can remember things for months,” said the keeper. “Ours know when it is feeding time. They swim to the top before we even open the food.” | Goldfish crowd at the top of a tank.
Two good sources agreed. Ella felt much better. The three-second memory was not a fact. It was a myth. | Ella smiles holding a notebook with a check mark.
At home, Ella tried an experiment. Each time she fed Sunny, she tapped the side of the tank gently first. | Ella taps the tank lightly before feeding.
After two weeks, Sunny swam to the top as soon as she tapped. He remembered! | Sunny swims up to Ella’s finger.
Ella told Jake what she found. “Next time you hear a fact,” she said, “check it with a good source first.” | Ella shows Jake her notebook.
