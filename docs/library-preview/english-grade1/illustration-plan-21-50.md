# Grade 1 English illustration plan

Produce artwork for the remaining 30 drafts in three batches of ten. Give each book four to five images total, including its cover. Use three story illustrations as the baseline and add a fourth where it shows a distinct step, contrast, or turning point. Keep each book as a draft in Library Studio so the artwork and reading layout can be previewed together.

Before drawing each book, make a reference sheet for recurring characters, clothing, places, and important objects. Use Western or U.S. surroundings when the story supports them and Cambodian surroundings when the setting can be localized naturally. Use a geographically neutral setting where place adds little. Keep the people and setting consistent across the book.

Use wide illustrations at the top or bottom for environments and action that fills the page. Place compact scenes or object close-ups on the left or right when they sit naturally beside the words. Use a different composition and story moment for every selected page, and use WebP for the artwork. Keep the other pages text-only. Preview both portrait and landscape layouts so the English text has room and no important details are cropped. Add exact text for signs or diagrams through editable Studio text where possible.

## Batch 1 · Lessons 21–30

Ten covers and three or four illustrated story pages per book. Lesson 27, *A Better Question for Bot*, is the example for this approach.

| Lesson | Book | Setting and illustration focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 21 | The Shade Moves | Cambodian schoolyard. Keep the tree and camera angle fixed across morning, noon, and afternoon so readers can compare the chalk marks and shadow lengths. |
| 22 | A Home on a Back | Neutral tropical coast. Show the land snail on a leaf and the hermit crab on the shore, with close-ups showing how the shells differ. |
| 23 | A Ramp for the Cart | Neutral classroom building corner. Keep the shelf height and blocks the same when showing the two ramps. |
| 24 | Rain on the Roof | Cambodian home in the rainy season. Trace the water from roof to gutter, down the pipe, and into the covered barrel. |
| 25 | Twenty Seeds, Two Rows | Cambodian home garden. Show exactly two rows of ten, then show one seed moved out of line while the total stays twenty. |
| 26 | The Quiet Seat | Neutral classroom reading area. Show the busy room, the calmer window seat, and the children taking turns. |
| 27 | A Better Question for Bot | Cambodian home courtyard. Show both wet cloths in similar airflow, with one in sun and the other in shade. |
| 28 | Pictures That Need a Second Look | Neutral science classroom. Show eight legs in the story’s flawed sample and exactly six in the corrected ladybug and vocabulary picture. |
| 29 | Bat at Dusk | Western-style porch overlooking trees. Keep the dusk light visible and distinguish the bat’s wings from a bird’s. |
| 30 | The Story Apron | Western-style family kitchen. Use the same apron and its deep pocket throughout. Show Eva helping at the worktop away from the stove. |

## Batch 3 · Lessons 41–50

Ten covers and three or four illustrated story pages per book.

| Lesson | Book | Setting and illustration focus |

| 47 | Who Came to the Pond? | Neutral freshwater pond. Keep the old tracks and newly observed duck tracks distinct so the clue and the direct observation remain clear. |
| 48 | We Can Fix the Directions | Neutral craft table. Keep the original folded sheet flat and clearly show the missing step that makes the paper tent stand. |
| 49 | The Evening Power Pause | Western-style family living room. Keep the battery lamp and book consistent. Show the bear at the gate inside the book’s illustration. |
| 50 | A Book for the Next Reader | Neutral classroom library. Show the frog waiting on the log because the bug is in its way, then show the recommendation card beside the book. |

For each batch, attach each image to its intended cover or page, open the Studio reader preview, verify placement and image loading, and review the details that the quizzes ask about before moving to the next ten books.
